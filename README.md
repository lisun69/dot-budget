# Dot Budget

A free, simple monthly budget tracker for Android with a resizable home-screen widget.
Dot-matrix design. Amounts in TK / BDT.

## Download
Open **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** on your Android phone and download **DotBudget.apk** (Android 8 or newer).
Allow "Install unknown apps" when asked. If Play Protect warns you, tap **More details > Install anyway**.

## Features
- **New salary** starts a new month. **Red +** (app or widget) adds a spend: event + amount.
- **Home**: money left, spent, % saved, days to payday, top events, recent spends. Shows your name.
- **Stats**: spending by event (month or all time), month-by-month spent vs saved chart, lifetime totals.
- **History**: all entries by month. Tap to delete.
- **Accounts (new in 1.1)**: save bank accounts (bank name, account number, branch) and cards (card holder, card number, bank). Copy or share them in one tap.
- **Widget (new in 1.1)**: AMOLED, Light and Clean themes, adjustable background opacity, your nickname. Resizable.
- **Setup**: your name and nickname, salary day, backup export/import (works with old Nothing Budget backups).

## Privacy
No account, no internet, no ads. All data stays on your phone. Never share your card PIN, CVV or OTP.
